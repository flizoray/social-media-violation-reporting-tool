# Social Media Violation Reporting & Account Safety Tool

ReportTool is a software project focused on social media violation reporting assistance, evidence documentation, account safety, and impersonation awareness.

The project is designed to help individuals, content creators, and businesses organise information about suspected social media violations and prepare documentation for appropriate reporting channels.

check: https://www.flizoray.github.io

## Overview

Social media users and businesses may encounter impersonation, fraudulent profiles, scams, harassment, and other potentially harmful online activity.

ReportTool focuses on making the reporting preparation process more organised through structured information, supporting evidence, and responsible reporting workflows.

The project supports legitimate reporting assistance and account safety education. It does not determine whether an account violates a platform's policies or guarantee that a reported account or piece of content will be removed.

## Features

The following capabilities describe the project's intended scope. Refer to the implemented application for the features currently available.

- Violation Documentation: Organise information about suspected social media violations.
- Evidence Management: Maintain relevant profile URLs, content links, screenshots, and incident notes.
- Report Preparation: Structure incident information into clear, readable report drafts.
- Account Safety: Provide resources for recognising suspicious activity and protecting online accounts.
- Impersonation Awareness: Help users document suspected fake profiles and impersonation incidents.
- Case Tracking: Maintain records of reporting progress and follow-up activities.
- Reporting Resources: Connect users with appropriate official platform reporting procedures.

## Supported Use Cases

### Social Media Impersonation

### Scam and Fraud Reporting Assistance

Organise information about suspicious profiles, fraudulent claims, and potentially deceptive online activity.

### Online Harassment Documentation

Maintain structured records of relevant incidents and supporting evidence for appropriate reporting channels.

### Account Safety and Recovery

Help users understand account protection practices and locate official account recovery resources.

### Brand Protection

Help businesses organise records of suspected impersonation and other social media incidents involving their brands.

## How It Works

The reporting assistance workflow is intended to help users:

1. Identify and describe a suspected violation.
2. Record the relevant profile or content URL.
3. Organise available supporting information.
4. Prepare a clear and factual incident report.
5. Locate the appropriate official reporting channel.
6. Record submission details and follow-up information where supported.

The workflow is intended for genuine incidents. Users should provide accurate information and avoid unsupported allegations.

## Responsible Use

ReportTool is intended for legitimate reporting assistance, documentation, and online safety education.

It is not intended to facilitate:

- False or misleading complaints.
- Coordinated harassment or abusive mass reporting.
- Unauthorised access to social media accounts.
- Evasion of platform security controls.
- Manipulation of moderation or enforcement systems.

Users should follow the applicable platform rules and submit reports only when they have a genuine basis for doing so.

ReportTool cannot guarantee enforcement outcomes. Each platform independently evaluates reports under its own policies and procedures.

Social media incident records may contain sensitive personal information.

Implement appropriate safeguards for any information processed or stored by the application, including access controls, secure file handling, and appropriate data-retention practices.

Users should never provide their social media passwords, authentication codes, or account recovery codes to a reporting assistance service.
